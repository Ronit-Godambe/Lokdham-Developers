Complete Guide to Buying Land Near Pune: 10 Things You Must Check Before Buying a Plot

Buying land near Pune can be an excellent long-term decision, but choosing the right property requires careful research. From location and connectivity to ownership records and development permissions, here are the important things you should check before purchasing a plot.

Read More ↓

Buying a plot of land is different from buying a ready-to-move home or apartment. A plot may look perfect during a site visit, but its actual value depends on several factors including its location, accessibility, documentation, permitted use and surrounding development.

If you are looking for land for sale near Pune, taking the time to verify these details before making a purchase can help you make a more informed decision and avoid unnecessary complications later.

1. Decide Why You Are Buying the Land

Before searching for a plot, first determine what you want to use it for. Your requirements will be different depending on whether you are purchasing land for investment, a future home, a weekend property or another permitted purpose.

Understanding your objective helps you evaluate the right location, plot size, accessibility and surrounding development.

2. Check the Location and Connectivity

Location is one of the most important factors when evaluating any property. A plot should not be judged only by its current surroundings. It is also important to consider its connectivity to nearby towns, major roads, employment centres, schools, healthcare facilities and other essential services.

When considering plots for sale near Pune, look beyond the distance shown on a map. Visit the location personally and understand the actual travel time and road conditions.

  • How easily can the property be reached?
  • What is the condition of the approach road?
  • How far is the property from the nearest major road?
  • What development is happening in the surrounding area?
  • How convenient will the location be in the future?

3. Verify Ownership and Property Title

Never purchase land without properly verifying who owns the property and whether the seller has the legal authority to transfer it.

Property ownership and title-related documents should be carefully examined before entering into a transaction. Where appropriate, buyers should seek professional legal advice to verify the title and identify any existing claims, restrictions or encumbrances.

Important: Property documentation can vary depending on the type and location of the land. Always have the relevant documents reviewed by a qualified professional before making a final purchase decision.

4. Check the 7/12 Extract and Other Land Records

In Maharashtra, land records can provide important information about a property. The 7/12 extract is commonly referred to when examining agricultural land records and can contain information relating to the land and recorded rights.

However, a single document should not be treated as proof of every aspect of a property's legal status. Relevant records should be checked together as part of proper due diligence.

Depending on the property, this may include checking records relating to ownership, mutations, survey or Gat numbers and other applicable documentation.

5. Understand the Land's Permitted Use

One of the most important questions before purchasing land is: What can legally be done on this property?

Land can have different classifications and permitted uses. The fact that a property is being marketed as a plot does not automatically mean that every type of construction or activity is permitted on it.

Buyers should understand the applicable land classification, permissions, zoning and development regulations before making a commitment.

6. Check the Approach Road and Accessibility

A beautiful plot is of limited practical value if reaching it is difficult.

During your site visit, check how the property connects to the public road and whether the access shown by the seller is actually available and documented.

  • Check the actual approach road.
  • Understand the road width and condition.
  • Confirm how the property connects to the surrounding road network.
  • Check whether access is clearly documented where necessary.
  • Visit the property in different conditions if possible.

7. Check Water, Electricity and Basic Infrastructure

Infrastructure can make a major difference to the practical value of a property.

Before buying, understand what infrastructure currently exists and what is actually planned. Do not rely only on verbal promises.

Depending on the project and location, buyers may want to understand the availability or planned provision of roads, electricity, water and other basic infrastructure.

8. Verify the Layout and Applicable Development Permissions

If you are purchasing a plot within a developed layout, ask to see the relevant layout and applicable approvals or permissions.

The plot shown to you on a brochure or marketing image should correspond with the actual property and relevant documentation.

This is another area where professional verification can be valuable before you make a financial commitment.

9. Understand the Total Cost, Not Just the Plot Price

The advertised price of a plot is not necessarily the complete cost of purchasing it.

Before finalising your decision, ask for a clear understanding of all applicable costs and charges associated with the transaction.

Depending on the transaction, these may include government duties, registration-related charges, documentation expenses and other applicable costs.

Knowing the complete financial requirement beforehand makes it easier to compare different properties fairly.

10. Visit the Property Before Making a Decision

Never make a land purchase decision based only on photographs, videos or an online listing.

Visit the property yourself. Walk around the plot, inspect the surrounding area and understand the approach road, nearby development and overall environment.

A personal site visit can reveal details that are impossible to understand from a brochure or property listing.

Red Flags to Watch Out For

While searching for land near Pune, be cautious if you come across any of the following situations:

  • Pressure to make an immediate payment.
  • Unclear ownership or incomplete documentation.
  • Promises that are not supported by documents.
  • Unclear access to the property.
  • Inconsistent information about the plot or its boundaries.
  • Unusually low pricing without a clear explanation.
  • Reluctance to allow proper document verification.
